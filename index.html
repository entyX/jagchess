Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. A school club can set up, invite members and use every feature without paying or entering a card.

Do students need an email address?

To join and play they sign in with Google or an email and password. To check in to a meeting they don't need an account at all: scanning the QR code is enough.

Can students still use lichess or Chess.com?

Of course. JagChess works alongside them. You can even run your club's lichess tournaments from inside JagChess.

Who can see our club?

Only members. If you make the club private it's hidden from search and people need your join code. Member details are never sold or shared.

What if I'm not a chess expert?

You don't need to be. The puzzle builder checks every move for you, and you can borrow whole puzzle collections other clubs have shared.

What JagChess asks for, and why.

Three permissions, each one asked for only when you choose to use the thing it is for.

Signing in with Google

We receive your email address and your name, and nothing else. They create your account, put your name on your club's leaderboard, and let your club's admins reach you. JagChess does not read anything else in your Google account.

Gmail, for club admins only

An admin who wants to send their club's newsletter can connect their own Gmail, so the message arrives from their own address rather than a stranger's. The permission is send-only: JagChess cannot read your mail, and nothing goes out until you press send.

Lichess, optional

Linking a Lichess account lets you join your club's tournaments there without leaving the site. Nothing is requested until you press Join, and the access token stays in your own browser.

Everything we store, who can see it, and how to have it deleted is in the privacy policy.
